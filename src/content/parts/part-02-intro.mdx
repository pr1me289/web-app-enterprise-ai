Three schools of spec-driven development have emerged. GitHub Spec Kit centers the spec itself. AWS Kiro centers the workflow — requirements, design, tasks. This work centers something neither addresses: the **governed translation from enterprise intent into agent-usable context**.

That translation is where enterprise AI actually lives or dies. Not at the model. Not at the prompt. At the layer between stakeholder intent and the evidence an agent is allowed to reason over.

The subject here is *the approach*. The vendor-onboarding scenario is how we demonstrate it — not the point of it.
