The subject is *the approach*. The mock business scenario — a fictional manufacturer onboarding a fictional vendor — is an illustrative vehicle, not the point.

The approach generalizes across enterprise workflows. What changes between workflows is the corpus and the agents; what does not change is the governance model and the shape of the pipeline.
