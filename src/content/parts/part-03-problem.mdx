Enterprise workflows are not free-form conversations. They have:

- **Role-based access** — not every agent, not every user, should see every document.
- **Policy over questionnaire precedence** — when a vendor-supplied answer disagrees with an internal policy, the policy wins.
- **Auditability by default** — every determination must be traceable to the evidence that produced it.
- **Temporal drift** — the "why" behind a decision often lives in a thread, a policy revision, or a precedent record outside the primary source.
- **Non-null discipline** — retrieval gaps must produce escalations, not hallucinations.

Naive RAG collapses these dimensions into a single similarity search. This section frames the alternative.
