Three findings from 2025–2026 research reshape the enterprise AI design space:

- **Context rot is real.** Across 18 LLMs, performance degrades as input length grows. Information buried in the middle of a long prompt is weighted less than information at the edges.
- **Multi-file agentic coding collapses.** Models that hit 87.2% Pass@1 on single-function benchmarks drop to 19.36% on distributed multi-file tasks — a 68-point cliff once work actually looks like work.
- **Instruction success is brutal.** Tsinghua's AGENTIF benchmark introduces ISR — the proportion of instructions for which *all* constraints hold. 9-of-10 compliance scores zero. Governance failures cluster where the model was "mostly right."

Meanwhile, a new product category — *context engines* — has crystallized in early 2026. Most of them are solving retrieval-over-code. Almost none are solving **governed translation of stakeholder intent into scoped, authority-aware agent context**.
