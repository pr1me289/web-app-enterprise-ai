80% of enterprises believe in the transformative impact of AI and have invested accordingly. 80% say they have not yet seen that impact in the bottom line. Both numbers are from the same year, and both keep holding.

The failure mode is not that the model is wrong. The failure mode is that the model was never given a system to be right *inside of*.
