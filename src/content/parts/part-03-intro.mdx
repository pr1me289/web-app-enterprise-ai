The subject is _the approach_. The mock business scenario — a fictional manufacturer onboarding a fictional vendor — is an illustrative vehicle, not the point.

The approach generalizes across enterprise workflows. What changes between workflows is the corpus and the agents; what does not change is the governed translation from enterprise intent into executable context.
